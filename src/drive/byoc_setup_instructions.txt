Bring Your Own Credentials - Setup Instructions
===============================================

Google only lets a small number of people sign in through Synchotic's own
Google app, and the rclone option is being retired. So to download everything,
including game rips and large chart packs, you make your own small Google app
and sign in through that instead. It takes about ten minutes in a browser, and
you only ever do it once.

Anywhere below that gives you a name to type, the name does not matter to
Google. Type exactly what is written and you cannot get it wrong.

Each step has a link. They all open in whichever project is picked in the
dropdown at the top of the page, so once step 1 is done, check that dropdown
says synchotic-byoc whenever you open a link.

A banner about "Cloud Hub" or a dashboard shutting down is about Google's own
home page, not these steps. Ignore it.

Instructions:

1. MAKE A PROJECT
   https://console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate
     Project name:  synchotic-byoc
     Location:      leave whatever it already says
   Click Create, wait a few seconds, then pick synchotic-byoc in the project
   dropdown at the top. Everything after this happens inside that project.
   (Google may add numbers to the end of the project ID. That is fine.)

2. TURN ON GOOGLE DRIVE
   https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/drive.googleapis.com
   Click Enable.

3. FILL IN THE APP INFO
   https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/overview
   Click "Get started".

   App Information
     App name:            Synchotic
     User support email:  pick your own address from the dropdown
   Audience
     select External
   Contact Information
     Email addresses:     your own address again
   Finish
     tick the box agreeing to the user data policy, then Create.

4. SAY WHAT YOU ARE ASKING FOR
   https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/scopes
   Click "Add or remove scopes". In the filter box paste:

     https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly

   Tick it, click Update, then Save.
   Google will call this a restricted scope and warn you. That is expected.
   Nothing is being sent anywhere, the app runs on your own machine.

5. FILL IN THE BRANDING
   https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/branding
   Google will not publish the app (next step) until these are filled in:
     App name:                   Synchotic
     User support email:         your own address
     App logo:                   leave empty
     Application home page:      https://noahbaxter.dev/synchotic/
     Privacy policy link:        https://noahbaxter.dev/synchotic/privacy.html
     Terms of service link:      leave empty
     Authorized domains:         noahbaxter.dev  (only if it asks)
     Developer contact:          your own address
   Click Save.

6. PUBLISH IT (DO NOT SKIP)
   https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience
   Under "Publishing status" click "Publish app" and confirm.
   It must end up saying "In production". If it says "Testing", Google
   throws away your sign-in every 7 days and Synchotic keeps logging you out.

   Google will then say "Your app requires verification" and ask you to
   submit it for review. Ignore that. It only matters for apps used by more
   than 100 people, and this one is used by you. The only thing being
   unverified costs you is one scary screen the first time you sign in.

7. MAKE THE KEY
   https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/clients/create
     Application type:  Desktop app
     Name:              leave the name it suggests
   Click Create.

   In the box that pops up, click "Download JSON" RIGHT AWAY. Google only
   shows the secret part of the key this once. If you closed the box without
   downloading, delete that client and make a new one.

8. INSTALL IT
   Rename the file you just downloaded to "credentials.json" exactly.
   Move it into this folder, right next to these instructions.

9. ENABLE IT
   In Synchotic go to Settings > Account > Mode and choose
   "Bring Your Own Credentials". It signs you in straight away: use the same
   Google account.
   Google will say the app is not verified. It is yours. Click "Advanced",
   then "Go to Synchotic (unsafe)".


FAQ
---
Why am I being signed out every 7 days?
   Step 6 did not stick. Open the Audience link again. If publishing status
   still says "Testing", set it to "In production" and sign in again.

Google will not let me publish.
   Something in step 5 is missing. Open the Branding link, fill in whatever
   it has flagged, Save, then publish again.

Why does Synchotic say it is not set up?
   The file must be named exactly "credentials.json", not "credentials(1).json"
   or "client_secret_....json", and must sit in this folder, beside these
   instructions.

The menu names do not match what I see.
   Google renames these pages every few months. "OAuth consent screen",
   "Google Auth Platform", "Branding", "Audience", "Clients" and "Data Access"
   are all the same area. The links above go straight to each page, so use
   them rather than the menus where you can.
